# Stirge original visual

Sprint 10's original Tiny Stirge geometry, UV atlas, and painted texture are
generated by `generate_stirge.py` and `paint_stirge_albedo.py`. The creature
has a compact reddish-brown body, six barbed legs, two pairs of scalloped
fleshy wings, and one long forward proboscis. It has no Wasp stripes,
antennae, or rear stinger. The scripts use no game art or third-party pixels.

Blender 4.5.10 LTS runs the painter with `--out
assets/flying-animals/stirge-albedo.png`, then the generator with
`--rig <local measured rig>`, `--albedo` set to that PNG, `--mesh-data
assets/flying-animals/stirge-mesh.json`, and local `--report`, `--blend-out`,
and `--fbx-out` paths. The measured Eagle rig is a private build input for
animation alignment. Its transforms, the editable Blender file, and the FBX
stay in ignored local evidence; only original geometry, weights, UVs, and
structural bone names ship. Runtime binding supplies the donor's live
bindposes to an instance-local mesh and material swap. A validation or attach
failure retains the donor.

The generated mesh has 512 vertices, 374 polygons, 15 bone groups, and at
most two bone influences per vertex. Its SHA-256 is
`449453e7dc3eb75cf32947b4c3c99cf1accc4b433c7e85c5a591a0ac3a6a800b`.
The 1024 px albedo SHA-256 is
`1c4bd9d75f6b6786e923b6e3004cdd9866a5149ba8f53118c4a7aab0ec0b1f7f`;
the mesh manifest pins that file and hash. Blender top and front renders were
inspected. These source renders do not establish live animation, scale,
attachment pose, material-controller behavior, or owner visual approval;
those require guarded in-game review before publication.
