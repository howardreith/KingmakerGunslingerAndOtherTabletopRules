# Musket fit-experiment sources

`musket-pass-through.fbx` is a Blender import/re-export derivative of the
preserved Mesh Masters *Flintlock Rifle* source at
`assets-source/third-party/models/mesh-masters-rifle-musket/source/Musket 01.fbx`.
Mesh Masters is credited under CC-BY-4.0; the original, license, attribution,
hash, and provenance files remain unmodified in that third-party directory.

`musket-minimal-control.fbx` and `musket-clearance-stock.fbx` are original,
project-owned clean-room diagnostic grayboxes made only from Blender primitives.
They use no official game mesh, texture, downloaded mesh, traced art, or
generative model. The repository license applies to those two candidates.

All three files are generated by `generate_musket_fit_candidates.py` under
Blender 4.5.10 LTS with `PYTHONHASHSEED=0`. The script also produces the `.blend`,
source-space renders, and machine-readable build report. Production FBXs contain
no camera or light.
