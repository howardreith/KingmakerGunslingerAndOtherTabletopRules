# v0.0.161 — Sprint 27: Griffon

Adds the adult Pathfinder first-edition Griffon to Summon Nature's Ally only:
IV summons one, V summons 1d3, and VI–IX summon 1d4+1. No Summon Monster,
celestial/fiendish template, new feat gate, hippogriff, or mount is added.

The Large magical beast has its printed five-HD chassis, bite and two talons.
Weapon Focus applies to the bite; each natural attack adds live Strength once.
A qualifying native charge with Pounce can add two conditional rake attacks;
an ordinary full attack has three attacks. Misses do not grant retries. The
printed adult has no special Grab, carry-off, Rend, or wing/tail attacks.
Existing lawful held-target rake restrictions remain shared behavior; Griffon
has no product grapple-initiation command.

Flight uses the established ordinary-map airborne route at 80 feet. The engine
representation does not separately simulate its printed 30-foot land mode,
Fly ranks/checks, maneuverability, selectable altitude, or terrain bypass.
Printed darkvision, low-light vision and scent retain the published passive
senses limitation. Racial skill bonuses, printed feats, trip defense and
five-foot melee reach use verified native consumers.

A project-owned hybrid eagle/lion body and painted summon medallion ship with
the creature. Native animation, selection, navigation and resource ownership
are retained. Coarse anatomy, rigid pointed wings and simplified transitions
remain visual debt. Owner aesthetic review: NOT_PERFORMED_NONBLOCKING.

The six new roots preserve all released identities and icon assignments.
The source catalog contains 111 project creatures, 1,108 generated roots and
29 retained native wrappers, totaling 1,137 choices. Monster 96/545 is unchanged;
Nature's Ally becomes 98 creatures/563 roots. Runtime qualification and exact
machine/working-fixture restoration are recorded in the Sprint 27 coverage.
