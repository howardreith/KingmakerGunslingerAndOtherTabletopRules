# v0.0.162 - Sprint 28: Hill, Stone, and Fire Giants

Adds adult Hill and Stone Giants to Summon Nature's Ally VI and Fire Giant to VII. Their quantity progressions provide eleven SNA-only roots through IX. They have no Summon Monster or celestial/fiendish variants.

All three are Large humanoids with distinct visible bodies and painted summon icons. They carry summon-owned weapons and armor where printed. Weapon attacks, unarmed slams, and ammunition-free rock throws use separate native attack modes. Hill and Fire throw one rock per prepared attack; Stone's Quick Draw permits its printed two-rock full attack. Fire's heated rock adds a separate fire damage component. Eligible giants can attempt to catch an incoming rock once per round before damage, with Stone's +4 limited to that catch check.

The native Kingmaker engine remains the source of ordinary hit rolls, full-attack action spending, range and cover, damage reduction, resistances, saves, buffs, and critical handling. Stone's +8 rocky-terrain Stealth is scoped to Hills and NaturalCaves area settings. Summoned equipment is isolated and cannot be transferred or looted.

The existing passive-sense limitations remain. Modest body color, proportions, animation seams, and clipping remain a visual backlog; the units are visible and usable. No owner aesthetic approval is recorded.
