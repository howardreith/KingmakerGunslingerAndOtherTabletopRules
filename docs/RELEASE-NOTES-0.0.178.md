# Tabletop Added Rules v0.0.178

This stable release adds one adult, untemplated Ice Devil (Gelugon) to Summon
Monster IX. It adds no Summon Nature's Ally route, lower-tier quantity,
alignment-template child, variant elemental or other creature.

The Ice Devil is a Large 14-HD controller with a distinct original insectile
body, spear and medallion icon. It uses a nonlootable +1 frost Large spear for
its printed iterative manufactured attacks, followed by its secondary bite and
tail. The spear retains its x3 critical multiplier, enhancement and separate
cold packet. Its owned gear is removed with its source and cannot become
transferable permanent stock; disarming it leaves the natural fallback routine.

Qualifying hits from that exact owner's spear or tail attempt its supernatural
Slow: Fortitude negates at a live Constitution-based DC (23 on the adult
profile), and failure applies the native Slow condition for 1d6 rounds. The
bite, misses, unrelated cold damage and another wielder do not carry it. This is
not poison and does not add a standalone Slow spell.

Cone of Cold and Ice Storm are available at will at caster level 13. Cone of
Cold uses its native cone, Reflex-half and spell-resistance consumers for 13d6.
Ice Storm preserves its separate physical and cold packets, no-save/SR rules
and native area/movement consequence; cold immunity does not erase the physical
packet. Its area visual does not add repeated damage that the installed spell
does not deliver.

Wall of Ice uses the explicitly selected bounded adaptation introduced for the
Bone Devil: one visible grounded 10-foot planar section per cast, with a
temporary owned navigation claim, native object damage, residual breach cold
and the recorded fire/fog branch. The Ice Devil's own caster level gives the
section 39 HP and a 13-minute duration. Breach, expiry, source removal, module
disable and teardown restore the claimed navigation state exactly. Different
owners and Bone/Ice sources do not release one another's claims. This adaptation
obstructs movement only; it is not line-of-effect blocking and does not offer a
player-drawn multi-section shape or hemisphere.

The creature also carries its own fear aura, regeneration 5 with the printed
good-damage suppression, constant airborne presentation at its 60-foot fly
speed, infernal defenses, and area-control AI that conservatively refuses unsafe
cold areas or walls. Kingmaker exposes one effective movement speed, so the
separate printed 40-foot land mode is not modeled or added to the fly speed.

## Disclosed omissions and exclusions

Persistent Image is omitted with the owner's explicit approval and no
substitute effect. The bounded installed-library capture found no established
Persistent Image, Major Image, Silent Image or figment graph; this disclosure
does not claim that faithful engine support is impossible. No decoy or extra
combat effect is invented.

As with the existing summoned roster, Greater Teleport and nested summoning are
excluded. The release does not invent an injury poison, fire vulnerability,
electrical protection or an extra free cast/full attack.

## Narrow v0.0.177 carry-forwards

The Nalfeshnee-owned Slow ability now uses the printed close-range derivation:
55 feet at caster level 12. This changes only that owned ability and preserves
its target count, save, spell resistance, duration and action effects; it does
not alter the native Slow spell, Glabrezu or the Ice Devil hit rider.

The Nalfeshnee Unholy Nimbus public wording is corrected from "Blindness" to
the native Daze condition it actually applies. The v0.0.177 tag and assets are
not replaced.

This release also contains the independently merged outsider visual and wall
presentation refinements from product PR #96, preserved as the current master
lineage rather than reset during Sprint 40.
