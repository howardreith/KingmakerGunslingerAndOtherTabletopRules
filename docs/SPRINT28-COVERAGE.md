candidate. The 67 earlier native executions retain their original source,
package and observer identities. They diagnose development behavior; they do
not qualify final bytes. A PASS requires its raw observation, exact package,
true child completion and restored transaction. A failed or missing observation
remains a failure or unknown.

Plan on roughly 2–5 minutes per ordinary guarded execution plus restoration,
and more for cold-load save sequences; the selected final campaign is likely
several dozen minutes. These are estimates from development transactions, not
timeouts or permission to skip cleanup. The corrected public candidate still
needs its full strict package gate before any final-byte native qualification.

The owner's only new gameplay-scope disposition is the named Climb/Craft
mapping omission for these summons: Hill Climb +10, Stone +12, Fire +14 and
Fire Craft (one) +8 remain printed references, not implemented named skills.
Native pit behavior is unchanged, and a focused pit/climbing task is backlog.
This may affect climbing performance and is not full skill fidelity. Generic
gameplay waivers, other mechanic failures and restoration exceptions remain
release blockers.
