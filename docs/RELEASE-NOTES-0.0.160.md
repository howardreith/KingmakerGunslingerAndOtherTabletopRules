# v0.0.160 — Expanded Summoning: Gorgon and Bulette

Adds six untemplated summon choices: Gorgon at Summon Monster VIII (one) and
IX (1d3); Bulette at Summon Nature's Ally VI (one), VII (1d3), and VIII–IX
(1d4+1). Gorgon is the project's charter-selected extension to the ordinary
Summon Monster table. Bulette follows the ordinary SNA VI placement. Existing
family alignment and duration conventions remain in effect.

Gorgon has its adult magical-beast profile, primary gore and secondary hooves,
separate live-strength Trample, and a 60-foot ally-aware petrifying cone. A
failed first exposure causes temporary stone with round-by-round recovery
saves. Another distinct Gorgon breath while that effect remains makes it
permanent without natural recovery saves. Native petrification immunity and
Stone to Flesh removal apply. Each Gorgon independently rolls its actual
1d4+1-round breath cooldown; duplicate callbacks are one exposure.

Bulette has its adult magical-beast profile, Savage Bite with the proper signed
Strength multiplier and 19–20 threat, and two primary claws. Leap makes a real
DC20 jump check during a legal charge: success replaces its charge attack with
four claws and no bite; failure retains the ordinary single charge attack.
Current skill, speed, reach, legal movement, action expenditure, interruption,
target loss and opportunity rules apply.

The selected ordinary-map adaptation is entirely above ground. Printed 20-foot
burrow movement remains unmodeled and is not added to land speed. No underground
navigation, terrain destruction or teleport is introduced. A faithful native
tremorsense consumer is unavailable, so tremorsense remains unsupported rather
than granting blindsight or unrestricted detection. Inherited sense limitations
continue at their existing scope.

Gorgon uses original project-owned armored-bull geometry on native animation;
Bulette uses the exact native adult body. Original painted summon medallions and
a Leap emblem follow the current icon pipeline. No proprietary mesh, texture or
animation is redistributed. Bodies are functional with rough proportions,
faceting, seams, secondary motion, texture detail and contact choreography still
in the visual backlog. Human aesthetic review: NOT_PERFORMED_NONBLOCKING.

All released creature identities, roots and approved icon pixels are preserved.
The completed public catalog contains 110 project creatures, 1,102 generated
placements and 29 retained native wrappers: 1,131 visible choices. Sprint26 adds
exactly two creatures and six public roots. No Sprint27 content is included.
