# Eastern Weapons Item Balance

Prices use the repository's transparent tabletop-compatible magic-weapon
method. A mundane weapon costs its family base price. Masterwork adds 300 gp.
Cold iron doubles the base weapon cost; a cold-iron magic weapon also pays the
ordinary 2,000 gp magic surcharge. Native effective bonuses use
`effective bonus² × 2,000 gp`. Bespoke effects have zero automatic enchantment
cost and instead add the fixed premium shown below.

Mountain-Sunder's fixed 20,000 gp premium owns both the non-native Mighty
Cleaving property and its Power-Attack-dependent once-per-round 1d6 force hit;
neither is disguised as an effective enhancement bonus. Unfixed Form's 12,000

polymorph or size-difference condition exists. The prices remain monotonic
within every family, early guaranteed weapons remain attainable, and all three
capstones are exact effective +10 weapons.
