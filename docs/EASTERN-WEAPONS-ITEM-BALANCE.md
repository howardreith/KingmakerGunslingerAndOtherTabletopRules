# Eastern Weapons item balance

## Pricing authority

Generic items use the established tabletop-compatible construction method.
Mundane price is the locked family base cost. Masterwork adds 300 gp. Cold iron
doubles the mundane weapon cost for these non-ammunition weapons. A +1 weapon
adds the 300 gp masterwork cost and the ordinary `2,000 gp x bonus squared`
magic-weapon cost.
