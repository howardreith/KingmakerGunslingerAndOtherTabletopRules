# Sprints 30–31 outsider/celestial batch — unreleased candidate

These are candidate notes. The five new species remain hidden until the scoped
mechanics are qualified; this document does not claim a release or native PASS.

The selected batch adds Lemure (Summon Monster II), Dretch (III), Hound Archon
(IV), Astral Deva (IX) and Trumpet Archon (IX), and upgrades the existing Lantern
Archon in place. It adds23 routes and preserves Lantern's seven existing routes,
identities and ordering. There are no Summon Nature's Ally additions.

Lemure retains its mindless two-claw routine. Dretch has independent finite Fear
and all-creature Stinking Cloud, with safe AI targeting. Hound has armed and
natural alternatives, Aid and archon auras. Lantern keeps distinct standard/full
light-ray actions and an owned luminous orb. Astral has its own protective aura,
disrupting hammer/two-hit stun and spell-like resources. Trumpet uses a genuine
CL14 prepared cleric book with bounded legal preparations and independent slots,
plus its owned sword/trumpet and archon-exempt paralysis blast. The intended
ordinary Cleave compatibility correction remains runtime-unqualified here.

Owner decisions retain general PF1 live attack-role Strength for Hound and general
supernatural DC scaling for Trumpet, disclosing their printed damage/DC
discrepancies. Offensive dismissal/banishment remains available; caster travel,
nested summoning and expensive-material equivalents are excluded. The existing
shared Magic Circle implementation supplies alignment AC/saves and configured
new-control immunity; summoned-creature contact/SR barriers and suppression of
existing control are outside that published adaptation. Future common Circle
improvements carry through its shared consumer.

No change shape, Lantern gestalt, alignment-feat roster, vertical-flight system,
general illumination/social/sense/skill framework or Sprint32 is included.
Named noncombat mappings and the legal prepared-loadout exclusions remain in the
batch ability disposition table. Combat skills and ownership/save safety remain
required. Functional-first visual acceptance permits rough anatomy, rigid wings,
seams, modest clipping and texture polish backlog. No personal aesthetic approval
is claimed.

Final evidence must distinguish fresh final-artifact public routes, persistence
and integration from specifically accepted prior development mechanics. All
original source/package/DLL/MVID/RunId tuples and verdicts remain intact.
