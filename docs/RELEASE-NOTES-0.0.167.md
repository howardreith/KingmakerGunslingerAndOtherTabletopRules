# Tabletop Added Rules v0.0.167 — Sprints 30–31

This batch adds Lemure (Summon Monster II), Dretch (III), Hound Archon (IV),
Astral Deva (IX) and Trumpet Archon (IX), and upgrades the existing Lantern
Archon in place. The five additions provide 23 new routes; Lantern retains its
seven existing routes, identities and ordering. There are no Summon Nature's
Ally additions.

Lemure retains its mindless two-claw routine. Dretch has independent finite
Cause Fear and Stinking Cloud, with safe AI targeting and an ally-affecting
cloud. Hound has armed and natural alternatives, Aid and archon auras. Lantern
keeps its light-ray actions and gains an owned luminous orb. Astral has its own
protective aura, disrupting hammer, two-hit stun and spell-like resources.
Trumpet uses a genuine CL14 prepared cleric book with bounded legal
preparations and independent slots, plus its owned sword/trumpet and a blast
that exempts archons. Astral and Trumpet retain ordinary Cleave's two-target
limit. Summoned equipment and future commands remain bound to their owners.

The batch follows general PF1 live attack-role Strength scaling for Hound and
general supernatural DC scaling for Trumpet (DC20 at its adult profile),
disclosing the discrepancies with printed species damage bonuses and blast
DC19. Offensive dismissal/banishment remains available; caster travel, nested
summoning and expensive-material equivalents are excluded. The shared Magic
Circle implementation supplies alignment AC/saves and configured new-control
immunity; summoned-creature contact/SR barriers and suppression of existing
control are outside that published adaptation. Future common Circle
improvements carry through the shared consumer.

No change shape, Lantern gestalt, alignment-feat roster, vertical-flight system,
general illumination/social/sense/skill framework or Sprint32 is included.
Named noncombat mappings and legal prepared-loadout exclusions are documented
in the batch ability disposition table. Functional-first visual acceptance
permits rough anatomy, rigid wings, seams, modest clipping and texture polish
backlog. No personal aesthetic approval is claimed.

The release evidence distinguishes final-artifact routes, persistence and
integration from accepted scoped development mechanics under their original
source/package/DLL/MVID/RunId identities. Original failed results are retained.
