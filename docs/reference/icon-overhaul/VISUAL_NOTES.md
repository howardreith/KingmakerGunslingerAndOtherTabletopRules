# Visual Reference Notes

These screenshots are **before-state references**. They show both the defects to repair and nearby Kingmaker/mod icons whose visual language should be matched.

## 01 — Rapid Reload in the main feat list

`references/originals/01_rapid_reload_feat_list.png`

Focus on the left panel. Rapid Reload is visible between Quick Draw and Shake It Off. Compare its icon against Shield Proficiency, Shake It Off, Skill Focus, and the other ordinary feat icons.

Desired correction:

- use the proper muted feat-icon red;
- enlarge the circular reload-arrow motif;
- enlarge and rebalance the tool slightly;
- remove the small blue corner decorations.

## 02 — Rapid Reload weapon sub-selection

`references/originals/02_rapid_reload_weapon_choices.png`

The list currently exposes Blunderbuss, Musket, Pistol, Revolver, and Rifle. The last two must be removed. The remaining three category icons currently use pale cards with one-off blue corner glyphs; replace them with the gradient-square decorative-monogram visual system.

## 03 — Weapon Focus with Blunderbuss

`references/originals/03_weapon_focus_blunderbuss_reference.png`

The left panel shows the current Blunderbuss parameter/category icon and several vanilla weapon-category icons. This is another surface that must use the corrected centralized firearm category icon.

## 04 — Weapon Focus with Musket, Pistol, and Nodachi

`references/originals/04_weapon_focus_musket_pistol_nodachi_reference.png`

Musket and Pistol show the current pale-card style. Nodachi demonstrates the successful decorative-monogram approach already used by the mod. Match that broad design language for Blunderbuss, Musket, and Pistol; do not simply copy the exact Nodachi colors without checking the established palette/template.

## 05 — Inventory/vendor item icons

`references/originals/05_inventory_weapon_icon_comparison.png`

The vendor list on the left contains ordinary Kingmaker weapon icons such as tridents, a two-bladed sword, wakizashi, and warhammers. The shared stash at upper right includes several mod-added weapons/firearms.

Use the ordinary Kingmaker item icons as the style baseline:

- transparent background rather than a dark rectangular card;
- weapon generally runs from lower-left to upper-right;
- weapon uses most of the available diagonal;
- small consistent safety margin;
- recognizable silhouette and clean alpha edge.

The firearm silhouettes are already reasonably diagonal; their conspicuous dark border/background is the primary defect. Eastern weapons and the Elven Branched Spear require a broader scale/orientation pass because several are too short within the canvas or too horizontal, especially Wakizashi and Elven Branched Spear.
