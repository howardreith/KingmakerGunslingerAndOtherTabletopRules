# Tabletop Added Rules v0.0.173

This stable release adds the Barbed Devil (Hamatula) and Bone Devil
(Osyluth) to Summon Monster. It exposes five untemplated routes: Barbed Devil
at VIII and IX, and Bone Devil at VII, VIII, and IX.

The Barbed Devil has its own spined body and icon, two primary claws, separate
Barbed Defense retaliation, claw fear, grab, and impale consumers, ordinary
Cleave/Power Attack behavior, two-ray Scorching Ray, Hold Person, and a
resource-limited Unholy Blight. The Bone Devil has a distinct skeletal,
insectile body and working sting tail, four equal-role natural attacks,
wounding-sting Strength poison, a fear aura, separate standard and quickened
ordinary invisibility, and its printed outsider defenses represented through
the supported Kingmaker systems.

Wall of Ice uses a bounded Kingmaker adaptation: each cast forms one grounded
10-foot section that owns temporary GridGraph movement obstruction, can be
damaged and breached, produces its residual cold/steam effect, and restores
the captured navigation state exactly when removed. It does not claim
line-of-effect blocking, player-drawn multi-section geometry, a hemisphere,
or Strength-based breaking.

Unsupported named spell-like abilities are omitted without substitutes.
Barbed Devil omits Major Image, Produce Flame, Pyrotechnics, and Order's
Wrath. Bone Devil omits Major Image and Dimensional Anchor. Summoned creatures
still cannot teleport, travel between planes, or create nested summons.

No Summon Nature's Ally entries, alignment-template variants, Ice Devil, or
Sprint 36 content are included. Existing summon identities and routes remain
in place.
