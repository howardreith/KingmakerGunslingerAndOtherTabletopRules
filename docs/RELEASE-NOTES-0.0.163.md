# Tabletop Added Rules v0.0.163 — Giant Humanoids II

Sprint 29 adds adult Ettin, Cloud Giant and Storm Giant to Summon Nature's Ally. Ettin begins at V and has five roots through IX; Cloud Giant begins at VIII and has two roots; Storm Giant has one IX root. The eight roots are untemplated, and Summon Monster, existing routes and the 29 retained native wrappers keep their identities and order. The source roster contains 117 project creatures, 1,127 generated roots and 1,156 visible choices when all eight new roots are published.

Ettin uses two independently owned flails or javelins. Its ordinary full flail attack has two attacks per arm, while a standard attack remains one attack. Cloud Giant uses an oversized morningstar without a wrong-size penalty, separate slams and its own 2d6 rock; it can catch rocks and use Great Cleave. Storm Giant uses a masterwork greatsword or +14 composite bow, catches rocks without acquiring a rock throw, and has separate Vital Strike and Improved Vital Strike actions. Its electricity immunity and constant Freedom of Movement are active. All summoned equipment stays with its owner and cannot become loot; arrows and javelins follow the bounded ammunition-free native convention for a short-lived summon, without transferable supplies or extra actions.

Cloud Giant has independent at-will Obscuring Mist and one-use Fog Cloud at CL16. Storm Giant has separate limited Call Lightning and Chain Lightning at CL15, baseline DC15/DC18, with finite Call Lightning bolt state. Each summon owns its uses. Cloud areas retain their native finite durations after the summon's end; Storm's activation and granted bolt end with its owner. Autonomous casting screens unsafe fog placement and immune or unreachable lightning targets, and each creature retains an ordinary attack fallback.

The scoped engine limitations are explicit. The omitted named skill consumers retain these printed reference values: Cloud Climb +19, Craft (one) +10 and Perform (string instruments) +8; Storm Climb +17, Craft (one) +13, Perform (sing) +12 and water-only Swim +22; Ettin Handle Animal +8. There is no rank reassignment or substitute bonus. Unsupported vertical-only levitation, swimming, water breathing and world-scale Control Weather are deferred; land movement and native pit behavior are unchanged. The installed static navmesh wall has no damageable HP entity, so Awesome Blow applies the printed 1d6 collision damage to the victim but cannot apply the wall object's own 1d6. No general weather, aquatic or vertical movement system was added. Summoning restrictions on nested summoning, planar travel and prohibited expensive-material effects remain.

The three creatures have original icons and recognizable native bodies, including Ettin's two visible heads. Coarse proportions, simple second-head motion, seams, clipping that does not block use, textures and choreography remain visual backlog. No owner aesthetic approval is claimed.
