# Tabletop Added Rules v0.0.175

This stable release adds the Vrock to Summon Monster. It exposes three
untemplated routes: one at VII, 1d3 at VIII and 1d4+1 at IX. No Summon
Nature's Ally route or alignment-template child is added.

The Vrock has a distinct vulture-headed winged body and icon. It fights with
five primary natural attacks — two claws, a bite and two talons — all at its
printed 10-foot reach, with DR 10/good, electricity and poison immunity, acid,
cold and fire resistance 10, and spell resistance 20. It carries Cleave,
Combat Reflexes, Improved Initiative, Lightning Reflexes and Power Attack, and
it has no Pounce, Grab or Rend routine.

Stunning Screech is a once-per-hour standard action: every creature except
demons within 30 feet must make a Fortitude save or be stunned for one round,
at a live Constitution-based DC of 21 at the adult profile, with spell
resistance not applying.

Spores is a free action every three rounds: every adjacent creature takes 1d8
immediately and then 1d4 each round for ten rounds as vines grow, with no save
and no spell resistance, matching the printed extraordinary ability. Adjacency
is measured from the actual footprints and is not the same as the Vrock's
10-foot melee reach.

Heroism and Mirror Image are each available once per summoning at caster level
12, using the installed native spells and two independent single-use resources.

## Owner-decided omissions, with no substitute effects

**Spores omits the printed holy-water interaction.** Its other three printed
counters work: casting bless on an affected creature destroys the growth, a
supported disease-removing effect strips it, and immunity to disease prevents
exposure entirely — including the opening 1d8 — or halts further growth if it
is gained after exposure. No faithful creature-directed holy-water application
was identified in the audited installed paths, so that one counter is
unavailable in this adaptation. This is a narrow accepted omission, not a
claim that all printed Spores counterplay is implemented, and no consumable,
substitute action or repurposed weapon enchantment stands in for it. Poison
immunity is not disease protection, and ordinary Dispel Magic is not the
disease cure.

**The Vrock's automatic Stunning Screech is deliberately cautious.** The
screech is a true area effect and can stun your own non-demon companions, so
left to its own judgement the Vrock withholds it whenever a non-exempt ally or
bystander stands in the radius, attacking or using another ability instead.
Beside your party it will often not screech on its own. You can still order it
yourself from the action bar under the ordinary standard-action and
once-per-hour rules, and that includes accepting the collateral. The effect
itself was not made enemy-only and your party was not given immunity.

**The demon exemption covers this mod's own demons.** A search of the
installation's loaded blueprints for subtype features found eighteen, including
devils and daemons, and none naming demons, so the exemption is resolved from
the registered set — Dretch, Shadow Demon, Succubus, Babau, Hezrou and Vrock.
A demon belonging to the campaign or to another mod is not recognised and is
not exempt.

**Movement.** Kingmaker exposes one movement speed, so the Vrock uses its
50-foot flying speed with airborne navigation and its printed 30-foot land
speed is omitted rather than summed.

**Not represented.** Telekinesis and Dance of Ruin. Summoned creatures still
cannot teleport, travel between planes, or create nested summons.
