# ADR-0010: License Original Project Work Under MIT

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-12

## Context

Sprint 2 creates distributable original source and documentation. Leaving that work without a license would make reuse and contribution rights ambiguous.

## Decision

License original Kingmaker Gunslinger source and documentation under the MIT License, attributed to Howie and Kingmaker Gunslinger contributors.

This grant does not apply to Kingmaker, Unity, Unity Mod Manager/Harmony binaries, referenced mod source, tabletop rules text, or future third-party assets. Each remains subject to its own terms and provenance record.

## Consequences

- Contributors and downstream users have clear permission for original project work under MIT terms.
- Any copied or materially adapted third-party source must retain its applicable notice in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.
- Asset packages continue to require per-asset licensing and attribution.
