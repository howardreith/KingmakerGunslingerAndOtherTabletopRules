# 0.0.172 — Bearded Devil and Kyton

Adds Bearded Devil (Barbazu) and Kyton (Chain Devil) to Summon Monster V–IX:
one at V, 1d3 at VI, and 1d4+1 at VII–IX. These are ten untemplated routes;
Summon Nature's Ally and existing summon identities remain unchanged.

- Barbazu uses its glaive or two claws as alternative attack modes. Both claws
  must hit the same opponent in the qualifying sequence to trigger its beard.
  Damaging glaive hits cause Infernal Wound: two bleed damage with the native
  spell-healing caster-level DC 16 check. Devil Chills keeps its day-scale onset
  and daily progression; ongoing owned effects end with their summon source.
- Kyton has four chain attacks, 10-foot chain reach, typed chain armor,
  cold immunity and regeneration 2 with its printed bypass sources. It does not
  inherit devil fire or poison immunity.
- Both creatures have distinct original bodies, real painted medallion icons,
  independent nontransferable equipment and exact-source cleanup.

The owner approved three engine adaptations. Dancing Chains is a standard
activation of the Kyton's own four existing chains, extending those weapons by
up to 15 feet. Activation makes no attack; subsequent native single/full
attacks use normal action costs. It does not control environmental or player
inventory chains. Unnerving Gaze is a targeted standard action at 30 feet, with
live Charisma DC, native fear/mind-affecting defenses and shaken for 1d3 rounds.
Players can target eligible allies; AI selects enemies. It replaces the passive
tabletop gaze/avert-eyes cycle. The unavailable Heal skill has no substitute
action; the printed Heal DC 17 treatment option is omitted by owner direction.

Unsupported named skills and passive/world senses remain documented reference
values, without broad substitute bonuses or a new world/skill subsystem.
Source/summoner area departure, death and expiry retire owned ongoing effects;
other sources' unrelated effects remain independent. No nested summons,
teleportation, campaign scripts, XP or transferable loot are added.

Rough texture seams, simple beard/chain motion and minor clipping remain cosmetic
backlog. No personal owner aesthetic approval is claimed. See the
[PF1 contract](../planning/SPRINT34-RULES.md) and
[qualification record](../planning/SPRINT34-STATUS.md) for exact execution
artifacts, original-source reuse, final coverage and restored-machine records.
Sprint 33 remains closed; no Sprint 35 creatures or historical release replacement.
