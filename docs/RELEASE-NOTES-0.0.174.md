# Tabletop Added Rules v0.0.174

This stable release adds Babau and Hezrou to Summon Monster. It exposes seven
untemplated routes: Babau at V through IX and Hezrou at VIII and IX. No Summon
Nature's Ally routes or alignment-template children are added.

Babau has a distinct lean, slime-coated body and icon. It supports separate
natural and nontransferable-longspear attack modes, native sneak-attack
qualification, constant See Invisibility, useful at-will CL 7 Dispel Magic,
and source-owned Protective Slime retaliation against natural or unarmed
attackers.

Hezrou has a distinct broad toad-like body and icon. It uses bite and claw
Grab without constrict, impale, or swallow riders, plus Power Attack and the
existing private Cleave/Great Cleave path. Its stench sickness and held-victim
nausea are separate source-owned effects. Its supported CL 13 spell-like
abilities preserve alignment, saving throw, spell-resistance, resource, action,
collateral, and melee-fallback behavior.

Three unsupported branches are omitted by explicit owner decision, with no
substitute effects: Protective Slime does not damage manufactured weapons or
ammunition, Babau omits Darkness, and Hezrou omits Gaseous Form. Protective
Slime never redirects weapon damage to the wielder. Summoned creatures still
cannot teleport, travel between planes, or create nested summons.

Existing summon identities, routes, wrappers, and ordering remain in place.
This release does not include Vrock or any Sprint 37 work.
