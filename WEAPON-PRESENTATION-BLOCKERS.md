# Weapon Presentation Calibration Blockers

## Hard blockers

None as of 2026-08-20.

## Open evidence gaps (not hard blockers)

- The unchanged baseline now has exact state-labelled stored and held-idle
  imagery for all 22 production variants. Combat-ready, attack/fire/thrust,
  reload, locomotion, equip/unequip transition, female, Small, and Enlarged
  coverage remain ordinary open work.
- Native donor axes are not yet represented as explicit semantic frames. The
  next diagnostic checkpoint must measure or otherwise prove them rather than
  infer them from total renderer bounds.
- The stale firearm donor readiness assertion is repaired and the guarded
  observer passes. Native donor axes still need explicit frame measurement.
- The authored service Pistol, Revolver, and Rifle source frames need actual
  mesh/marker proof. Existing unexplained transforms are not acceptance data.
- Medium female, Small, and Enlarged cosmetic fixtures may require narrower
  request-local character construction. Lack of first-pass coverage is not a
  stop condition.

## Rejected stopping rationales

The initial Eastern observer timeout, stale firearm observer expectation,
missing screenshots, and current transform uncertainty are ordinary
engineering tasks. No protected save was selected or overwritten, and no
launch-environment safety condition was encountered.
