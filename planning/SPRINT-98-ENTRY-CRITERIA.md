grant it with Reload through Firearm Proficiency. Availability remains exact:
one equipped production Blunderbuss, one loaded round, and a non-Wrecked
effective condition. The ability delegates delivery to the Sprint 97 native
transaction and accepts a unit only as the cone direction anchor.

until a save-free guarded runtime scenario proves the registered blueprint and
