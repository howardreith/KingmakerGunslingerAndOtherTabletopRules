# Sprint 80 entry criteria: comprehensive save-free acceptance

Compose one guarded scenario from 30 already-qualified synchronous save-free
vertical slices covering level-20 progression, evaluated chassis, level-up and
multiclass commit, grit lifecycle/recovery, production equipment switching,
bonus feats, Gun Training, and every runtime-qualified deed branch.

Each slice retains its existing exact assertions and deterministic cleanup. The
aggregator prefixes every assertion with its slice identity, fails closed on an
exception, and requires exactly 30 slices. Exclude Startling Shot, Targeting
Head, detached respec, lifecycle corruption, scatter, Targeting Arms, and
Death's Shot because their attempt or design-authority boundaries remain active.

No inventory, save, UI, vendor, or campaign mutation is permitted beyond the
already-qualified exact rollback fixtures embedded in individual slices.
Require focused aggregation checks, inherited validation, the complete domain
suite, runner/preflight checks, clean Release build, strict packaging, exact mod
load, and two consecutive fresh-process PASS runs.
