# Sprint 20 entry criteria

Sprint 20 follows one of two branches.

## Token carrier passes

Proceed to consumable Black Powder Charge and Lead Ball item blueprints only after all of the following are observed in Kingmaker:

- Firearm Proficiency grant succeeds and the Test Musket equips.
- A-D token fixture creates exactly three non-default tokens and one token-free firearm.
- Save, exit to desktop, restart and reload retain all four states.
- Native Heavy Crossbows remain unmarked and unaffected.
- No crash, item-price anomaly or duplicate token appears.

## Token carrier fails

Remain on persistence. Preserve the exact failure log and determine whether dynamic item enchantments are omitted, rebuilt, stripped or duplicated by Kingmaker serialization. Do not begin ammunition transactions until a per-item carrier survives the lifecycle.
