## giant-lineup phase

The disposable `DefaultPlayerCharacter` clone initially has no race. The
observer assigns only that clone the exact native Human race and verifies
both its progression race and Human fact. It explicitly records this verified
Human as a **six-foot art anchor**, not a mechanical native height assertion.
It never applies scale calibration or changes gameplay size/reach/collision.

The request-owned caster receives a real CL20 Wizard spellbook and memorizes
exact canonical parents. SNA access is explicitly fixture-granted: this is
cast-path/art evidence, not proof that a Wizard naturally learns SNA. The
phase preserves the real parent/selected-child/prepared-slot links and records
book identity, slot before/after count, actual cast/spawn event counts, exact
unit identity and caster-owned native lifecycle provenance. It uses the same
qualified synchronous command/animation advancement as the existing targeted
spellbook route fixture; it does not claim natural click/cast timing.

The six simultaneous lineup actors come from single canonical SNA casts:
Hill VI, Stone VI, Frost VII, Fire VII, Cloud VIII and Storm IX. Generated
giants have no SM roots; the observer checks that fact instead of inventing
one. It separately casts the retained Frost Giant through its real evil SM
VIII wrapper, measures that actor, and cleans only its exact owned instance.
No giant is produced by a debug spawn or direct `RuleSummonUnit` substitute.

Before placement, the observer surveys bounded walkable native graph lanes
for seven points at four-metre spacing, with at most 0.4-metre projection gap
and at most 0.05-metre ground-height spread. It moves only request-owned
actors. If the first area lacks a comparable 24-metre lane, that is a precise
fixture failure/unknown; retain it and select a narrower justified observer or
appropriate guarded fixture. Do not relaunch the unchanged failing phase.

Spawn/fader/dissolve state must settle naturally. No renderer, material alpha,
shader, dissolve or appearance buff is forced. Four actual idle samples,
separated by twelve Unity frames, record:

- Exact actor, blueprint, prefab, view instance, lifecycle caster and remaining
  duration; view/local/world scales and transform matrices.
- Every skinned renderer's name, enabled/active state, selected/excluded body
  classification, renderer-to-view and renderer-to-world matrix, materials,
  shader/render queue/known blend-dissolve properties and instance identities.
- Body classification from **positive anatomy skin weights**, not merely a
  full donor bone palette. Hand-only weapon skins are excluded even if their
  palette contains all native body bones. Unskinned effects are excluded.
- Named live head/foot/toe bone points in world and view space.
- Transient `SkinnedMeshRenderer.BakeMesh` crown/sole and whole body height in
  the exact recorded common world-up basis. Native vertices, indices and skin
  weights remain in memory and are never serialized/exported.

Kingmaker's Unity version has a documented BakeMesh scale ambiguity. The
observer compares both candidate Bake-to-world conventions against transient
weighted `bone.localToWorld * bindpose * sourceVertex` skinning. It records
both maximum errors and accepts a convention only within 0.005 world units.
This avoids deriving anatomy from renderer/culling bounds. Every temporary
baked mesh is destroyed in `finally`, including error paths.

Standing comparison eligibility requires valid samples, stable view identity
and local scale, no more than 0.05-unit pose-height variation and no more than
0.15-unit sole/ground gap. Raw height ratios and the explicit six-foot art
comparison are descriptive; failure cannot authorize later calibration. The
observer records eligibility and **calibrationApplied=false**.

`cleanup2-six-giants-human-lineup.png` renders the actual live bodies and native
materials/lighting from one restored native camera pose. The camera faces
perpendicular to the surveyed row, frames the measured body span and verifies
all crown/sole points, screen fade and nonblack luminance. It uses no proxy,
replacement shader, synthetic silhouette or copied donor mesh object. The
camera pose/render targets and all temporary image resources are restored or
disposed in `finally`. Pixel content still requires actual visual inspection.

## menus phase

The phase requires idle closed action groups and no preselected ability. It
temporarily registers/selects only the disposable caster, prepares the nine
canonical SNA parents in its new spellbook, opens the **real native spell
group**, chooses each real level tab, and opens its actual variant popup.

Each popup is checked against the exact current parent's child identities,
including unknown third-party entries. Native scroll positions cover the
entire content range with overlapping pages. A row counts as covered only if
its entire rectangle was visible inside the actual viewport on a captured
page. Every page uses `NativeIconScreenEvidence.Capture` to retain the full
native framebuffer and currently rendered text/icon/child/spellbook links.
This is not a live-sprite facsimile. Reachable nested quantity/variant groups
are recursively opened through their native group control; a missing control,
cycle, unexplained row difference or incomplete page coverage fails explicitly.

Popup scroll positions and owned popups are restored/closed in `finally`.
The phase attempts every party, selection, group-toggle and pointer restoration
even if an earlier step throws, then reports aggregated restoration errors.
Original party spellbooks' memorized slot object references, spell object
references and availability are compared exactly. Only the disposable
caster's newly prepared slots are changed; no saved hotbar entry is rewritten.
An empty original memorized-slot set is recorded as such, not old-save proof.

Nine-tier capture coverage does not by itself prove semantic redundant-choice
removal. The native evidence must be inspected against MENU-01's identity-based
replacement policy. Druid spontaneous conversion, alternate adapter access,
old learned/prepared/pinned Elemental compatibility and module-off restoration
remain separate acceptance work and are explicitly excluded from this phase's
coverage assertion.

`ExpandedSummoningViewScalePatch` use of the root does not prove safety either.

The minimal safe target is an **observed** common visual child containing the
included body renderers, positively weighted skeleton, animation manager and

that subtree. The old census has bone parents but lacks complete transform

subtree with per-axis factors justified by the common-plane measurements and

collider radii/heights and AI unchanged. Native soft-collider centers may still
follow the transformed torso; verify actual selection/movement/usability and
