# Sprint 36 entry criteria: core deed bundle

## Authority

- `AUTONOMOUS-GUNSLINGER-MISSION.md` continuous completion contract.
- Mission-authorized local `GUNSLINGER_PFSRD.md`, level-one deeds.
- `planning/GUNSLINGER-FIDELITY-MATRIX.md` approved Kingmaker adaptations.
- Runtime-qualified Sprint 35 grit resource, persistence, rest, recovery, and
  duplicate-protection checkpoints.

## Authorized scope

Deliver the coherent first-level deed bundle required by the roadmap:

- Deadeye;
- Gunslinger's Dodge;
- Quick Clear;
- reusable deed availability, native grit spending, player-facing actions,
  progression grants, diagnostics, and guarded runtime acceptance.

Deadeye is a declared-before-firing adaptation. It must preserve native range
penalties and spend exactly one grit per range increment beyond the first.
Gunslinger's Dodge must document a safe Kingmaker reaction representation and
retain its armor/load prerequisites. Quick Clear must target the exact equipped
misfire-broken firearm, require positive grit for the standard form, and spend
one grit only for the move-action form.

## Required gates

- Exact stable blueprint identities and progression ownership.
- Per-unit and per-attack deed state; no unrelated-unit or unrelated-item leak.
- Atomic grit/action mutation and duplicate-event protection.
- Focused tests, repository validation, complete domain suite, clean Release
  build, strict package validation, exact mod load, and two independent guarded
  runtime PASS runs for each changed mechanical boundary.

Sprint 36 is a checkpoint, not a stopping condition.
