## Deliberate release choices

- Stirge remains implemented but is omitted from public summon options. Its working drain is not justification to keep it published against the owner's decision.
- The owner chose removal of Wall of Ice after observing it. Do not spend more time recovering the old investment.
- Dust Mephit's ice-like visual is acceptable for now.
- Stegosaurus/Tyrannosaurus and other dinosaur awkwardness was accepted, sometimes affectionately. Do not standardize it away.
- The general persistent summon sheen is a shared styling exception, not permission to erase real buffs or intrinsic creature effects.
- The prior visual closeout's “adequate” judgments do not supersede the new Nalfeshnee, Glabrezu, and Ice Devil feedback.
- The new Ice Devil screenshots remove the old lack-of-image baseline problem on DATA. They do not change host restrictions on named automated tests.

## Preservation checks

Use one or two controls for a changed shared mechanism. Examples: an unaffected summon option for publication; Lion/Hell Hound for activation; a native portrait control for UI; Elephant/Aurochs for Trample; Storm Giant for Fire Giant's local feat change.

Do not treat every listed creature as a mandatory new native run. Preserve accepted work unless the changed dependency creates a concrete reason to check it.
